## About Us

**Sigma Software Solutions** is an IT solutions and technology services company based in India, offering comprehensive technology solutions to businesses of all sizes.

We specialize in **IT infrastructure, custom software development, Point of Sale (POS) systems, IT hardware and peripherals, Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), and professional IT consultancy**.

Our focus is on delivering reliable, cost-effective, and business-oriented technology solutions that simplify operations, improve efficiency, and support long-term business growth.
