## About Us

**Sigma Software Solutions** is an IT solutions and technology services company providing comprehensive IT products, software solutions, technical support, and consultancy services to businesses of all sizes.

We specialize in **custom software development, Point of Sale (POS) solutions, IT hardware and peripherals, IT infrastructure, and Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) services**.

Our goal is to help businesses streamline operations, improve productivity, and maintain reliable technology environments through cost-effective and dependable IT solutions.

## Our Services

- **IT Solutions & Infrastructure:** IT infrastructure setup, installation, configuration, troubleshooting, and technical support.
- **Custom Software Development:** Tailor-made business applications, web-based software, management systems, and enterprise solutions.
- **POS Software & Solutions:** Point of Sale software, billing systems, retail management solutions, installation, configuration, and ongoing support.
- **IT Hardware & Peripherals:** Supply, installation, maintenance, and support of computers, printers, barcode scanners, billing printers, POS terminals, and related IT equipment.
- **Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC):** Preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, software support, hardware servicing, and ongoing IT infrastructure maintenance.
- **IT Consultancy:** Professional IT consulting, technology planning, system upgrades, infrastructure optimization, and business technology solutions.
- **Business IT Support:** Technical assistance, system administration, network support, and IT maintenance services for businesses and organizations.

## Our Commitment

At **Sigma Software Solutions**, we focus on delivering reliable technology, quality service, and long-term customer support.

We aim to be a trusted technology partner by providing businesses with complete IT solutions — from hardware and software implementation to ongoing maintenance and technical consultancy.
